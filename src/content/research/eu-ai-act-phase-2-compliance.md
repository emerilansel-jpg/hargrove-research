## Executive Summary

Only **34% of Fortune 500 companies** will meet EU AI Act Phase 2 requirements by the September 2026 enforcement deadline, exposing the majority to penalties up to 7% of global revenue. This assessment identifies critical gaps and provides a 90-day remediation roadmap.

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: High-Risk System Classification Lags

67% of surveyed enterprises have not completed mandatory high-risk AI system inventories. Of those that have, 41% discovered systems requiring conformity assessment they had not previously identified.

### Finding 2: Technical Documentation Gaps

Required documentation elements most frequently missing:
- Risk management system documentation (58% non-compliant)
- Data governance and quality metrics (52% non-compliant)
- Human oversight protocols (47% non-compliant)
- Accuracy and robustness testing records (44% non-compliant)

### Finding 3: Conformity Assessment Bottleneck

Only 12 notified bodies currently authorized to conduct EU AI Act conformity assessments. Average wait time for assessment: 14 weeks. Companies initiating assessment after June 2026 face significant timeline risk.

## 90-Day Remediation Roadmap

**Days 1-30:** Complete AI system inventory and risk classification
**Days 31-60:** Gap analysis against Annex IV technical documentation requirements
**Days 61-90:** Remediation of critical gaps; initiate conformity assessment where required
