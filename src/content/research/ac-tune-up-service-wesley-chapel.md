**Methodology**
---------------

Hargrove Research Group used a 100-point comparative framework designed for homeowners, property managers, and small commercial buyers seeking HVAC support during persistent late-season heat. Research was compiled from public web materials, consumer-facing service disclosures, state-license claims where presented, public review indicators, and the supplied federal energy, weather, and maintenance data. The review window was March–April 2026 for baseline vendor-screening design, with weather and public-facing vendor information refreshed through September 29, 2026.

No provider submitted a paid application, supplied proprietary operational data, or influenced the criteria. Because granular, consistently auditable data such as median response time, complaint rates, completed-job volumes, and realized invoice totals were not available for every organization, scores should be read as comparative public-information assessments rather than precise measures of technical quality.
